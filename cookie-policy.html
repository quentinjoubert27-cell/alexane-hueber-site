What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored by your browser when you visit a website. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in comparable ways.

What this website uses

This website does not use advertising or audience measurement cookies, and it does not track you across other websites. That is why it does not ask for your consent when you arrive.

The only stored information is strictly necessary: when an authorised person logs in to the administration area, the login session is kept in that browser's local storage. Other visitors are not concerned.

Third-party services

The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which involves a connection to Google's servers. See our Privacy Policy for more information.

Changes

If we add cookies that require your consent, such as audience measurement, we will update this page and ask for your consent first.

Managing cookies

You can view and delete stored data at any time in your browser's settings.
